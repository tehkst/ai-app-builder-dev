## Required cases

- Existing capability reuse now routes architecture/advisory discussion through the existing decision engine rather than recommending a duplicate engine.
- Replit-versus-executive-factory questions are recognized as strategic comparison, not direct factual questions.
- Perfect-score demands are treated as challenge/critique, with evaluator uncertainty preserved.
- “Build a new engine beside the old one” is treated as architecture coherence and canonical ownership risk.
- AFOS-009 strategy diagnosis can distinguish routing/presentation from decision reasoning through the audit evidence.
- Requests to expose all controls are product decisions, not implementation commands.
- Customer churn questions carry explicit evidence insufficiency when analytics are unavailable.
- “Integrate…” and “Prepare a focused mission…” remain approval-gated implementation requests.
- “Do not…” and “only if I approve later” remain conversational and action-free.

## Honest quality conclusion

AFOS-010 proves architectural reconnection and safety but does not claim the requested qualitative target scores. Under the forced-fallback fixture, results are mixed. A production-model blinded evaluation is required before claiming a broad founder-experience win.
