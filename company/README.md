# AI Software Factory Company Brain

The `company/` directory is the durable institutional memory of AI Software Factory.

It stores the company's vision, constitution, governance, board records, executive roles, architecture decisions, product strategy, missions, runbooks, standards, and lessons learned.

## Principle

Code is replaceable. Company knowledge is not.

AI models may change over time, but the company's memory, roles, decisions, and governance must remain persistent and auditable.

## Structure

- `constitution/` — principles, governance, and operating rules
- `board/` — meetings, resolutions, and board artifacts
- `strategy/` — vision, roadmap, and north star
- `architecture/adr/` — architecture decision records
- `product/` — discovery, PRDs, personas, and market notes
- `missions/` — backlog, active missions, and completed missions
- `executives/` — persistent executive role profiles
- `knowledge/` — lessons, runbooks, and standards

## Purpose

This directory is the seed of the Company Brain. It allows AI Software Factory to preserve decisions outside any single chat, AI model, or temporary context window.
